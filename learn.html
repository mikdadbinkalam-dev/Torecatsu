Learning paths

Follow a path from top to bottom. More articles are added over time, and every path says what is still missing.

BEGINNER

Science foundations

  1. Why is the sky blue? (light and waves)
  2. What is a black hole? (gravity and stars)
  3. Ask TORE-AI: "what is DNA", "what is photosynthesis", "what is pH"

Safe experiment: shine a torch through a glass of water with a drop of milk in a dark room and watch the light scatter (adult nearby).

BEGINNER TO INTERMEDIATE

Maths for builders

  1. Ask TORE-AI: "pythagoras", "what is a prime number", "derivative"
  2. How does encryption work? (primes in real life)

Practice: write 10 prime numbers by hand, then multiply two of them and try to factor the answer.

BEGINNER TO INTERMEDIATE

Computer science and AI

  1. What is machine learning?
  2. How does a mesh network work?
  3. Ask TORE-AI: "what is an algorithm", "what is the internet"

Project: write a tiny program that guesses a number between 1 and 100 using as few tries as possible (binary search).

INTERMEDIATE

Privacy and cybersecurity basics

  1. How does encryption work?
  2. What is a zero-knowledge proof?
  3. Read the Community Guidelines: only test systems you own or have permission to test.

Safe practice: use a password manager, turn on two-step login on your own accounts, and learn to spot phishing messages.

Planned next: more physics, chemistry and biology articles, and a beginner Python path.
