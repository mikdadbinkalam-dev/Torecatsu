Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what the TORECATSU website collects and why. It describes what the site actually does today. It is not legal advice, and we do not claim compliance with any specific law or certification.

Who we are

TORECATSU is a student-led online community. You can reach us through the contact form.

What we collect

  • Account (optional). If you create an account we store your email address, your password (only as a salted hash handled by our login provider, Supabase, so we cannot read it) and the display name you choose.
  • Member list. Your display name, member ID (like TRC-101), level and join date are shown in the public member list. Your email is never shown there.
  • Join and contact forms. Name or handle, email, field of interest, message and optional link. These are delivered through Netlify Forms (or Formspree, if we enable it).
  • On your device. We use your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage for settings such as sound, music, language, light/dark theme and whether you have seen the intro. Supabase also keeps your login session there. We do not set advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Server logs. Our host, Netlify, may log technical data such as IP address and browser type for operating and securing the service.

What we do not do

  • We do not run advertising or analytics scripts on this site.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • TORE-AI answers from a knowledge base that runs inside your browser. Your questions to TORE-AI are not sent to us or to an AI company.

Third-party services

The site loads code, fonts and icons from Google Fonts, Tailwind CDN, jsDelivr, cdnjs (Cloudflare) and Font Awesome. These providers can see your IP address when your browser requests those files. Hosting is by Netlify, and accounts and the member list are stored by Supabase. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

Why we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and show you in the member list.
  • To read and reply to your messages and applications.
  • To keep the site working and protected from abuse.

How long we keep it

Account and member data is kept until you ask us to delete it. Form messages are kept only as long as we need them to reply or manage the community.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your data (including your account and member-list entry) through the contact form, using the topic "Privacy request". You can also clear your browser storage at any time.

Children

TORECATSU is meant for students and learners. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you think a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Connections use HTTPS and passwords are hashed by our login provider. No website is perfectly secure, and we have not had an independent security audit. If you find a problem, please tell us through the contact form.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
