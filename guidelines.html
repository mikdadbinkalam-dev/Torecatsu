Community Guidelines

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Our slogan is "Know the system. Break the system." It is about understanding systems and building better, open ones. It is never about harming people or breaking the law.

Be respectful

  • Treat everyone with respect. No harassment, hate or threats.
  • Disagree with ideas, not with people.
  • Keep it friendly to beginners. Everyone starts somewhere.

Stay legal and safe

  • No hacking, scanning or attacking systems you do not own or have permission to test.
  • Cybersecurity topics are for learning and defence. Do not post stolen data, malware or instructions meant to cause harm.
  • Do not share anyone's private information.

Be honest

  • Say what you know and what you do not. Mark ideas as ideas.
  • Give sources for scientific claims when you can.
  • Do not claim partnerships, results or achievements that are not real.

Contributing

Want to help? Write or improve a learning article, suggest a project idea, report a mistake, or help translate to Bangla. Send your idea through the contact form with the topic "Contribute". Contributors may be invited to the Architect level when that program starts.

Reporting a problem

If you see something that breaks these guidelines, or find a security problem, use the contact form with the topic "Report". Please do not publish a security problem before we have had a chance to fix it.

Moderation

Maintainers may remove content, edit display names that break the rules, and suspend or delete accounts. Serious or repeated problems can lead to removal from the community. If you think we made a mistake, contact us and we will review it.
