// WHO WE ARE

We do not follow the system.
We create a new one.

TORECATSU is an organisation of curious people. We study how the world's systems really work - money, information, identity, networks, education - and when a system is closed, unfair or broken, we do not simply obey it and we do not simply complain about it. We design and build a better one that is open, transparent and suitable for all.

KNOW THE SYSTEM. BREAK THE SYSTEM. BUILD THE NEW SYSTEM.

What does "we do not follow the system" mean?

It does not mean breaking laws, harming people or creating chaos. Breaking a system, for us, means understanding it so deeply that we can see where it fails the people it is supposed to serve - and then replacing that part with something better.

Most of today's systems were designed for a few, by a few. They depend on gatekeepers, hidden rules and permissions. A student without money cannot enter the best classroom. A person without the right paper cannot prove who they are. A village without a tower has no internet. We believe these are design problems, and design problems can be solved.

So the TORECATSU method is simple: learn it, question it, rebuild it in the open.

Our principles

01 / OPEN BY DEFAULT

Knowledge, code and research are shared publicly. If only a few people can understand or check a system, it is not a fair system.

02 / SUITABLE FOR ALL

A system is only good if it works for the poor and the rich, the city and the village, the expert and the beginner. We design for the person with the least, first.

03 / PRIVACY IS A RIGHT

People should prove what is needed and nothing more. We build with zero-knowledge ideas, minimal data and no hidden tracking.

04 / NO SINGLE POINT OF CONTROL

Systems that depend on one company, one server or one person can be shut down or abused. We prefer decentralised, resilient designs.

05 / LEARN, THEN BUILD

We respect science. Ideas must be tested, measured and improved. Curiosity first, ego last.

06 / RESPONSIBLE FREEDOM

Our work must never hurt people. We follow the law, report weaknesses responsibly and use our skills to protect, not to attack.

The new system we are creating

Knowledge for everyone

Free, clear science and technology learning - physics, chemistry, biology, mathematics, computing and more - explained in simple language, including in Bangla.

Identity you control

Sovereign Passport: a way to prove who you are, or that a fact about you is true, without handing your whole life to a stranger.

Networks that cannot be switched off

Nexus Hyper-Mesh: community-owned wireless networks that keep working in disasters, outages and censorship.

Intelligence that serves people

Project Chronos, Vortex Agent DAO and TORE-AI: open, understandable AI tools that help people instead of watching them.

Fair value exchange

Disruptive economics: a way for people to cooperate and trade value without needless middlemen taking a cut or deciding who may take part.

A computer you can trust

Null-OS Sovereign: a small, transparent operating system with zero telemetry, so your device works for you.

How we work

  1. 1.Observe - find where a system fails real people.
  2. 2.Understand - study the science, the code, the rules and the incentives behind it.
  3. 3.Design - propose an open alternative and publish the design for criticism.
  4. 4.Build - prototype in the lab, test on a small network, fix what breaks.
  5. 5.Share - release it to everyone, document it, teach it, and let the community improve it.

Questions people ask

Is TORECATSU against the government or the law?

No. We follow the law. "Break the system" means challenging bad design with better design - not breaking rules or harming anyone.

Do I need to be an expert to join?

No. Beginners are welcome. Curiosity and a willingness to learn matter more than degrees.

Does it cost money?

The Initiate level is open access. For anything else, please ask through the contact form.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are handled by the login service in scrambled form. We collect only what is needed, and your email is not shown publicly.

How can I help?

Join, learn, share what you know, translate, test, write code or simply tell a friend.
